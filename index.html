Free and open source · For macOS 14 and later

Native Mac apps, made with care.

Slopapp is a growing collection of small, native utilities for the things you do every day: clipboard history, screenshots, opening links in the right browser, shrinking images.

Every app is free and open source. No subscription, no account, no tracking.

Principles

  • Free

    No trial, no subscription, no paid tier. Every app costs nothing, now and later.

  • Open source

    MIT-licensed Swift, developed in one public repository. Read it, fork it, improve it.

  • Private

    No account, no analytics, no tracking. Your clipboard, files and screenshots stay on your Mac.

The Slopapp launcher

One small app that installs, updates and removes every app in the collection.

Open the disk image and drag Slopapp to Applications. It's signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens like any other app.

Built in the open

We study how the best Mac utilities work, then build our own, natively, in one public repository. Found a bug, or want an app that isn't here yet? Open an issue or send a pull request.
